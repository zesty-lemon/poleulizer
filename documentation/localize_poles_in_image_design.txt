We need to take our bounding boxes and our located poles and map one to the other

We have bounding boxes drawn around each pole in each image. We also have locations of each pole,
as well as bearings drawn between the camera car and each pole. We can map the degrees (ex 50 degrees) to predicted
centers in the image using math.

Using the FOV and predicted bearings to the poles, we take the haversine distance between the projected
location and the location of bounding boxes, and assign one to the other.


Points of consideration:

Poles may be too far away yet show up in bounding boxes
 - This is probably fixable by just finding the minimum size and cutting boxes smaller than this
 - Poles are all around the same height, so if we figure out the height/width of a pole at its max distance
   and then just remove all boxes smaller than this it will be good enough.

Poles on the other side of the road will also have bounding boxes
 - We may have a scenario where poles are present on left and right sides of the road (we only care about right)
 - We cannot just remove all poles left of center - scenarios like car going around a curve may have right side
   poles visible on the "left" side of the image but not the left side of the road itself.
 - It's possible that if we just enforce close matches this might solve itself
